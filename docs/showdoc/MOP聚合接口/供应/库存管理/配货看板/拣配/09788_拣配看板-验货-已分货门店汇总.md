- POST
- RequestBody

|:---- |:---|:----- |----- |
|createDate|否|Object||
|--start|否|DateTime||
|--end|否|DateTime||
|whsOutNo|否|String||
|pickStatusList|否|List||
|printStatusList|否|List||
|companyIds|否|List||
|itemTypefCodes|否|List||
|specIds|否|List||
|createIds|否|List||
|pickStatus|否|String||
|itemTypeIds|否|List||
|whsId|否|String||
|merchantsTypeCode|否|List||
|id|否|String||
|pageSize|否|Integer||
|pageNo|否|Integer||
|sortField|否|String||
|sortDirection|否|String||
|viewId|否|String||
